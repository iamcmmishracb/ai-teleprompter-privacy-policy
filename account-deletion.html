Account & Data Deletion

App: Float Teleprompter (package: com.floatprompter.app)  |  Last updated: August 31, 2026

Float Teleprompter lets you create an account using Firebase Authentication, with account-linked data stored in Firestore. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, submit the form below and we'll process your request — you do not need the App installed on your device to do this.

Submit a Deletion Request

This form is powered by FormSubmit — your request is emailed directly to us at learneducamy@gmail.com. The first time this form is used, FormSubmit will send a one-time confirmation link to that inbox, which must be clicked once to activate delivery.

Alternative: Email us directly

You can also email learneducamy@gmail.com directly with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", including the email address or account ID used to sign up.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Firebase Authentication account (email, UID, login credentials)Yes — permanently
Account-linked data stored in Firestore (profile info, preferences)Yes — permanently
Recordings, scripts, and search history saved locally on your deviceNot affected — delete these yourself in-app, or by uninstalling the App / clearing app storage
Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics or crash logs already reportedMay be retained in de-identified form for diagnostic purposes

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification. You will receive an email confirmation once deletion is complete.
