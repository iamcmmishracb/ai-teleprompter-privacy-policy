Data Deletion

App: Float Teleprompter (package: com.floatprompter.app)  |  Last updated: November 7, 2026

The current version of Float Teleprompter does not have accounts. Sign-up and sign-in have been removed, and the App does not collect your name or email address. Your recordings, scripts, and history are stored only on your device, so you can delete them yourself at any time by removing them in the App, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App.

If you created an account in an earlier version of the App, you can request deletion of that account and its associated data using the form below. It will open your own email app with everything pre-filled, so the request is sent from your email address. You do not need the App installed on your device to do this.

Request Deletion of an Earlier Account

Clicking Submit opens your device's default email app with a message addressed to learneducamy@gmail.com, pre-filled with the details above. You'll just need to hit Send from your own email account to complete the request.

Alternative: Email us directly

You can also email learneducamy@gmail.com directly with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", including the email address or account ID used to sign up.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Earlier account data (email, display name, account ID, and any data linked to the account)Yes — permanently, once your request is verified
Recordings, scripts, and search history saved locally on your deviceNot held by us — delete these yourself in-app, or by uninstalling the App / clearing app storage
Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics or crash logs already reportedMay be retained in de-identified form for diagnostic purposes

Timeframe

Requests for earlier accounts are processed within 30 days of verification. You will receive an email confirmation once deletion is complete.
