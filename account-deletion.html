Privacy Policy

App: Float Teleprompter (package: com.floatprompter.app)  |  Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Float Teleprompter ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the App. We built the App to work with as little data collection as possible — most features run entirely on your device. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • No account required: The App has no sign-up or sign-in. We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, or password.
  • Search topics: When you type a topic or niche into the Ideas or Script Generator tabs, that text is sent to public, free third-party services (Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo) to fetch relevant content. We do not attach your identity to these lookups.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Advertising identifier (Android Ad ID): Used by Google AdMob to show ads and measure ad performance.
  • Device and diagnostic data: Basic crash and performance data may be collected to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

c) Information stored only on your device

  • Camera and microphone: Used to record teleprompter videos. Recordings are saved to your device's local storage and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Generated scripts and search history: Saved locally on your device so you can view or delete them later. We do not have access to this data.
  • Floating overlay: The "display over other apps" permission is used only to draw the teleprompter window on your screen. The App does not read, capture, or record the content of other apps or your screen, and no overlay data leaves your device.
  • Storage access: Used only to save and retrieve your recorded videos and scripts from your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — serves ads within the App. Google Privacy Policy
  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) — public content used to generate topic ideas and scripts. Wikimedia Privacy Policy
  • DuckDuckGo — public search results used for topic ideas and script content. DuckDuckGo Privacy Policy
  • Reddit — public trending posts shown for a given topic/niche. Reddit Privacy Policy

These services may independently collect data such as IP address or device information when the App communicates with them. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not upload your recorded videos to any server.
  • We do not use AI providers or send your data to AI APIs — topic ideas and scripts are generated from public web content, not personal data.
  • We do not monitor, read, or collect what you do in other apps while the floating overlay is shown.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, or files unrelated to the App's recording features.

5. Data Retention

Recordings, scripts, and search history remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We do not keep copies of this data on our servers.

6. Accounts & Data Deletion

The current version of the App does not support accounts, so there is no account to delete. All recordings, scripts, and history are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete them in the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

If you created an account in an earlier version of the App, you can ask us to delete that account and all associated data at any time, whether or not the App is still installed. See our Data Deletion page or email learneducamy@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email address you used. We will complete the deletion within 30 days of verifying your request.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke Camera, Microphone, or Storage permissions at any time in your device Settings — some features will not work without them.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads via your device's Ads Settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • You can delete locally saved scripts, history, and recordings directly within the App or by clearing app storage in device Settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
